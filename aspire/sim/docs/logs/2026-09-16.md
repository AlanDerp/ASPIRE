**The load-bearing new fact is the negative `hang`.** Every release height in `transport.md` had been
built as `support + hang`, assuming `hang > 0`. A slab *thinner than the finger pads*, pinched with
the pads pressed to the deepest pose the IK accepts, puts the pads *below* its base — so on all 15
seeds `hang = (hand_at_grasp − FINGERTIP_OFFSET) − box["bot"]` came out **−0.017…−0.019**, and the
release was commanded to hand z **0.215–0.222** against a rim of 0.141–0.142. The entry records that
the sign is not the point but carrying it is: read `hang` as a *definition* (the payload's underside
is at `hand − FINGERTIP_OFFSET − hang`) and the same three lines serve a 14 cm carton and a 15 mm slab
with no branch.

**A second fact that is not in the program: the walls are a `np.clip`, not physics.** `solve_ik`
clips its position argument to `[-0.1, -0.5, 0.005] … [0.75, 0.5, 0.9]`
(`cap/integrations/franka/libero_reduced.py:383`) and re-tries a fixed orientation ladder before
reporting failure, so an unreachable far-x command can settle into a *fallback* orientation with only
a `logger.warning`. Written up with the three consequences that matter to a fix program: the
0.107 offset is `DEFAULT_TCP_OFFSET` and therefore portable across tasks; a wrist tilt moves the pads
off the hand axis rather than defeating the wall; and a **successful `solve_ik` does not mean the
requested orientation was honoured**.

## libero_goal_task / put_the_bowl_on_the_plate — Stage 0 + Stage 1 (GPU 6)

**Runtime goal, not the identifier:** `env.handle.task_language` = *"Put the wine bottle on the
plate"* on 15/15 dev seeds. The identifier says *bowl*; it is a dispatch key and appears nowhere in
the program. Same lie pattern as the spatial and object suites.

**Result: 15/15 on dev seeds 51–65 with `initial_code.py`, and 15/15 with `fix_code.py`.**
No dev seed failed, so Stage 1 had no failure to triage and no seed was retried; every episode
succeeded on the first carry attempt with `carries=0`. The two "fixes" below are latent defects
found by direct measurement (scratch probes 1–5 under
`/mnt/nimloth/aspire_scratch/libero_goal_task/put_the_bowl_on_the_plate/`), each masked on all 15
seeds by a descent backstop that happened to fire in the right place.

1. **Inverted TCP-offset sign.** `pads = pos + R@(0,0,+0.025)`, so the inverse is
   `cmd = p - R@(0,0,+0.025)`; `initial_code.py` used `+`. Every descent was therefore commanded
   50 mm too deep. Visible only in the *height the pads actually reached*: the grasp close landed at
   pad_z 0.0906–0.0907 on all 15 seeds — the kinematic floor measured at 0.0874 — instead of the
   intended neck pinch at 0.1076–0.1155. Fixed; probe5 measured the corrected form to within 6.6 mm
   (commanded 0.200/0.120/0.100 -> measured 0.2000/0.1244/0.1066). The earlier probe3 check that had
   appeared to validate the pad model was circular — it computed the "measured" value with the same
   model under test.

2. **Release height governed by the backstop rather than the target.** The old
   `plate["floor"] + 0.004 + base_off` sums two independent camera percentiles, whose noise adds.
   Replaced with `z_release = z_pinch_achieved - 0.003` (the measured pad z at the close), which
   cancels all depth bias: the object's underside was on the support at that instant, so returning the
   pads there returns the base to support level. Release-pad_z spread across the 15 seeds fell from
   **30.5 mm to 8.1 mm**; the reverse travel below the pinch went from {5.7–6.3 mm x12, 35.1–36.2 mm
   on seeds 56/64/65} to {7.5–7.6 mm x12, 13.8–14.3 mm on 56/64/65} — a 29 mm bimodality collapsed to
   6 mm. No seed's reward changed.

Not added, deliberately: a wrist-tilt reach-wall escape (dev plate x max 0.7263 vs a measured wall at
0.750, ~4 sigma) and any control decision gated on a post-release segmentation.
