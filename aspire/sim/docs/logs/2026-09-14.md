**Mechanic that works: top-down pinch of the raised band + wrist yaw sweep with the
TCP held fixed.** A top-down orientation has `R[:,2] = (0,0,-1)` for every yaw, so
re-issuing `goto_pose([tx,ty,gz], topdown(yaw0 + k*10deg))` is a pure in-place
rotation about the vertical axis — the pinched lever turns with the wrist and the
control is never translated. Pinch point = the band's own median XY, `gz` = band's
median z − 8 mm (fingers clear the wide base by ~2 cm), closing yaw derived from the
band's minor axis so the fingers land on its two broad faces. 120 deg world-CCW in
10 deg steps turns the stove on. Measured: `gripper_width` 0.3036 (gap 0.0243 m) after
the pinch and 0.0171 m after the sweep, on **all 15** seeds; epilogue keyframe shows
the burner glowing red. Whole episode = **28 API calls** (1 pinch, 12 sweep steps).

   (0.773/0.621) and `can of tomato sauce` (0.641/0.436) rank the *same, wrong* can first — the score
   is tracking apparent size. A single-can reward probe settled identity: placing only the can at
   `(0.402, -0.083)` → reward **1.000**; only the can at `(0.752, 0.027)` → **0.000**. The goal object
   is the can whose label is blue. `blueness = mean(B) − mean(R)` over the mask pixels separates
   **+1.0 (target) vs −14.2 (distractor) on all 15 seeds** — a 15-grey-level gap with no overlap and
   no threshold to tune. This is a second instance of the same-suite pattern the butter worker found
   with a thresholded red fraction; the generalization is "choose the colour axis from the packaging,
   and prefer the signed channel difference when no single channel level is stable across candidates".
2. **Ladder every descent into ≤2.5 cm hops.** `goto_pose(..., z_approach=0.12)` makes one 12 cm jump
   from z 0.172 to 0.052; the arm landed at **z 0.080**, 2.8 cm short, and closed on air
   (`gap = 0.0012 m`). Split into ≤2.5 cm hops starting from the *measured* TCP, the same descent
   reaches the target and the grip holds at **gap 0.0635 m** — matching the can's 6.1 cm diameter, on
   grasp attempt 0 for all 15 seeds (`gap 0.0633–0.0637`). The yaw/height-fraction fallback in
   `grasp()` is therefore inert.
