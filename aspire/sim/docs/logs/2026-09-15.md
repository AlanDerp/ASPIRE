This failure presents as "the bowl rolled off the plate", so it is easily misdiagnosed as a release-height or plate-centre problem. Flipping to `release_xy = place + off` flipped seed 51 from 0.0 to 1.0.
- **Root cause 2: the point-cloud median is ~2 cm off for a hollow bowl.** The visible cloud is dominated by the far inner wall, so the median sits toward the camera (seed 51 median x=0.640 vs true centre 0.659). Kept the closed-loop residual correction (`shift = shift - err`) as a safety net.

## Fix loop: `libero_spatial_swap/pick_up_the_black_bowl_on_the_ramekin_and_place_it_on_the_plate` (GPU 6)

**Stage 0** — `initial_code.py` (retargeted from the sibling `..._between_the_plate_and_the_ramekin...` program): **13/15** on dev seeds 51-65; seeds 51 and 61 reward 0.000. Neither failure was a grasp failure — both lifted the bowl and failed on placement accuracy.

**Root causes** (two, both platform-level, not task-specific):
1. **SAM3 mask bleed through a same-class support**: the mask of the bowl stacked on the ramekin runs down through the ramekin (bowl-class) onto the table — measured `zlo = -0.005` vs a clean `+0.028`. This biased the in-plane centre 1-2 cm (fingers shoved the bowl: seed 52 (0.434,0.212) -> (0.403,0.286)) and inflated `tcp_to_bottom` by ~3.5 cm, so the release happened 3.5 cm above the plate and the bowl was dropped. Present on **13/15** seeds (only seed 58 had a clean mask).
2. **Non-reproducible carried grasp offset**: seed 51 pinch offset (+0.049,0.000) vs carried (+0.013,...) -> release column 3.6 cm past the plate, landed 0.038 m off. Seed 61 (0.000,-0.048) vs (-0.032,-0.035) -> landed 0.043 m off. The program's self-check reported 0.005/0.002 m of error on both, i.e. the error is invisible in the commanded frame (commanded TCP attained to <5 mm).

**Fixes** (`fix_code.py`, 786 lines):
- **A** — clip the stacked bowl's cloud at the support's measured rim (`sup = p90(ramekin pts z)`; `if d_ram < 0.09 and goal["lo"][2] < sup - 0.004`), then recompute `cloud_geom`. Lines 717-727. Fired on 14/15 seeds.
- **B** — `servo_bowl_over` (lines 481-522, called at 596): servo the *held* bowl's measured centre over the goal, then descend from `tcp_xy()` rather than the commanded column. `held_bowl_xy` (lines 450-477) with the **height gate** `z_floor = z_tcp - 0.20` (lines 463/473).
- The height gate was not optional: without it the servo locked onto the **decoy bowl on the table** (same class, `servo[1..4] bowl=(0.690,0.220)`), dragged the payload to y=-0.075 and released 0.134 m off (seed 51 reward 0.000). Second iteration with the gate: seed 51 reward 1.000.
- Also hardened target selection: screen "on the ramekin" against the **ramekin's own measured rim** (`ON_RAMEKIN_SLACK = 0.012`), never a scene-level `table_z` — a naive `table_z = min(lo[2])` was poisoned by the wooden cabinet (`zlo = -0.049`), which marked both bowls elevated and sent every grasp to the decoy.

**Result** — `fix_code.py` **15/15** on dev seeds 51-65 (all reward 1.000, no regressions). Seeds 51 and 61 flipped 0.000 -> 1.000 (landing 0.021 m / 0.010 m).

**Artifacts** — `$TASK_DIR/{initial_code.py,fix_code.py,findings.md,task_analysis.md}`, `outputs/working_codes/libero_spatial_swap_pick_up_the_black_bowl_on_the_ramekin_and_place_it_on_the_plate_fix.py` (byte-identical to `fix_code.py`). No blocked seeds. Held-out seeds 1-50 and the validation runner were **not** run (protocol).

**Observed, not fixed**: `radial_-x_high` (first in the release-column ordering) air-grasps on 13/15 seeds; the candidate fall-through handles it, and with the corrected centre the failed pinch no longer displaces the bowl. Worth noting for future tasks: with a servo-driven release the release-column ordering no longer affects accuracy, so candidate order could be chosen purely for grasp reliability.
